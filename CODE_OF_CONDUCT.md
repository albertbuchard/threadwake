# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

Threadwake welcomes careful, respectful contributions from people with different backgrounds, disciplines, and levels of experience.

We want discussion to make the work easier to understand, test, and improve. Disagreement is useful when it addresses the idea, evidence, or tradeoff without attacking the person.

## Expected conduct

- Be clear, specific, and respectful.
- Critique claims and implementation, not a contributor's identity or motives.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Make room for questions and different levels of familiarity.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, attribution, and intellectual-property rights.
- Use synthetic examples instead of exposing another person's work or data.
- Follow the project's security-reporting process.
- Stop behavior that a moderator identifies as disruptive or unsafe.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory abuse.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or repeated bad-faith disruption.
- Publishing private information, credentials, private conversations, or sensitive work records.
- Encouraging unauthorized access, destructive testing, or evasion of safety controls.
- Misrepresenting project status, evidence, affiliation, endorsement, or authorship.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release spaces, and other project-managed community channels. It also applies when someone represents the project in a public setting.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/albertbuchard/threadwake/issues) for an ordinary conduct concern, but do not post sensitive personal details. If a concern cannot be described safely in public, open only a minimal issue asking a maintainer to establish a private contact route, then wait for that route before sharing details. You may disengage from the interaction at any time.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct-reporting route.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, pause discussion, limit participation, reject a contribution, or exclude a participant when needed to protect the community and project. Responses should be proportionate, explain the relevant conduct boundary when safe, and avoid disclosing more information than necessary.

The project does not promise a moderation response or resolution time.
